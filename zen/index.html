How it works

One pipeline runs every morning for each active project — no manual effort until the PM reviews the result.

Why it matters

A PM managing 5–7 projects can lose an hour a day to writing summaries, chasing action items, and prepping status decks. Automating the mechanical half of that work — while keeping a human review step before anything reaches the team — gives that time back without losing accountability.
